## Stage 03 scope
- Existing saved offers, versioned Package ID, owner capability and separate read-only public share capability are reused. Public HTML/JSON and OG image omit private pricing, owner keys and messages. Native iOS share includes a personalized local PNG and public URL; OG uses a generic branded cover. Public web view is read-only, not a copy-and-book flow. Saved packages restore from same-device Keychain, not cross-device account sync. Repricing and current accepted quote precede booking. Conversion to Booking ID checks owner and booking access, matching selections, dates, party and price. A failed link can be retried.
- Package-scoped client/staff chat and accommodation request; save gate precedes chat; localized editable draft and manager decision (approve/alternative/decline). Approval alone never authorizes over-capacity: verified live Nuitee supplier rate/room/occupancy and current matching scope required; stale or sandbox quotes and other unverified suppliers remain blocked. Manager must offer an alternative or use a verified supplier path. Approval is not global.
- Visa submission/refusal tracked in audit; new v2 bookings gate ticket preparation/publication until all visa requirements are ready. Business purchase task after issued visas, client stage progress. This does not purchase tickets, submit visas externally or move money. Live issuer integration, fresh supplier availability/price confirmation and any required customer consent remain necessary before actual purchase.
- Separate client refund estimate screen and whole-booking cancellation request; internal Business estimate and existing staff recording/reconciliation workflow. Visa before submission refundable, submitted nonrefundable by agreed default; refusal, errors, partially delivered services, unknown pickup, supplier cancellation rules, provider fee and unverified/partial payment require review. Transfer 24 hours and $100 maximum late fee before start, otherwise manual review where facts are unknown. 9% proportional to refundable amount is the proposed estimate policy; 2% paid fee refund depends on provider, not automatically refunded. No automatic bank refund or separate service cancellation executed by this patch. Do not represent estimate as guaranteed payout.
- Compact distribution inside summary, no separate room allocation screen; adult limit warning remains independent of children; actual supplier capacity checked. No new full-repository delivery or replacement workflows.

## Validation / rollout
Backend core, FlightHub, Package Engine and migration suites run in local environment, with new tests for public sharing/access, room approval, visa stage and refund estimates. Swift syntax parsing and existing iOS regression scripts are local checks, not a full SwiftUI build. Xcode archive, real device visual QA, production Cloudflare deployment and live provider transactions not performed. Verify staging, CI, account/guest access, actual supplier terms and the proposed refund policies before production. Public links require deployed backend and correct canonical domain routing.
