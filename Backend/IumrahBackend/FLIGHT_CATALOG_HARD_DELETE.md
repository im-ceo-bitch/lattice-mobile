# Flight catalog hard delete — 2026-11-25

Scope: staff-curated flight offers, their legacy copy, Business sync feeds and transient search/calendar caches. No archive table is created. Booking records, issued tickets, price/financial audit records in FlightHub and its foreign-key relationships are intentionally untouched.

- Existing unified Worker cron `*/5 * * * *` invokes the independent D1 cleanup on minutes 00, 15, 30, 45 (UTC). No Ignav or other provider calls from this job. Does not change the existing provider-monitor schedule or workflow.
- The deletion cutoff is the actual departure timestamp for any leg of the offer, including the outbound of a complete journey. A date-only legacy record is expired after its airport-local departure day; exact valid timestamps take precedence. The cleanup uses hard DELETE and compare-and-swap guards against concurrent updates.
- Canonical/legacy curated rows and snapshots (including disabled feeds) are cleaned. Valid remaining cached itineraries stay available. Export, read, quote resolution, curation and legacy recovery reject expired records. Empty canonical export is authoritative; it does not resurrect a legacy or snapshot catalog. Date-only calendar cache is cleaned after its date/TTL, not prematurely within the same day.
- No migrations or new secrets are needed. The existing Business ZIP installer is unchanged. Upload the root-ready `iumrah-business-*.zip` to main, let **Apply iumrah Business ZIP update** finish, then manually run **Deploy iumrah backend**. Cron runs on the deployed Worker independently of Business app; D1 writes are not executed by ZIP extraction.
- Check Worker logs for `FLIGHT_CATALOG_HARD_DELETE` (counts only, no customer data). Download a fresh JSON from Business after a quarter-hour tick; departed rows should be absent. Previously exported files are not changed retroactively.
